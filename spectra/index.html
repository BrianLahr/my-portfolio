Measurement notes, privacy and iPhone tips

Pitch is periodicity, not a formant. YIN detects approximately 65–1050 Hz, with a causal observation window near 100 ms. Very noisy or polyphonic sources may show “no stable pitch”, octave errors or false notes.

H2–H8 / H1 is not THD. Normal voices have harmonics, which are strongly influenced by vowels, microphone response and vocal tract resonances. Do not use this measurement to certify a vocal effect or vocal safety.

Privacy: all microphone samples stay inside this browser session. No recording, upload, analytics or external fonts/scripts. HTTPS and a user gesture are required for microphone permission. The app stops microphone capture when leaving the foreground.

iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Open as Web App. Safari may request microphone access again on later launches. Use headphones if another app is playing sound.
